AniTimer Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

In short: AniTimer does not collect, store, or share your personal data on any server of its own. The app talks directly to AnimeSchedule.net, and everything it saves (sign-in tokens, cached images, your list data for the widget) stays on your device. There are no analytics, no ads, and no trackers.

Who we are

AniTimer is an independent iOS app for browsing the weekly anime airing schedule and managing your anime list on AnimeSchedule.net. AniTimer is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by AnimeSchedule.net.

Data the app accesses

AniTimer accesses the following data through the official AnimeSchedule.net API:

  • Public anime data — weekly timetables, anime details, posters, and search results. This does not involve any personal data.
  • Your AnimeSchedule account data (only if you sign in) — your anime list (statuses, episode progress, scores, notes), your public profile avatar, banner, and list statistics.

Signing in is optional. Without signing in, the app only fetches public anime data.

How sign-in works

Sign-in uses the industry-standard OAuth2 authorization flow (with PKCE) provided by AnimeSchedule.net. Your AnimeSchedule password is entered only on the AnimeSchedule website inside a secure system browser session — AniTimer never sees or stores your password. The app receives access tokens, which are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only (they are not synced or backed up off-device by the app).

What is stored on your device

  • Sign-in tokens — in the iOS Keychain, until you sign out.
  • Cached images — anime posters cached for up to 7 days (max 50 MB) to reduce network usage.
  • Widget data — your favorited/tracked anime and their poster images, stored in the app's shared container so the Home Screen widget can display upcoming episodes.
  • Preferences — settings such as dark mode, schedule timezone, and widget air type.

All of this is deleted when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate our own servers — no data about you is ever sent to us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data to train AI or machine learning models.

Third-party service: AnimeSchedule.net

All anime and account data comes from AnimeSchedule.net, and requests made by the app go directly from your device to their servers. Their handling of your account and data is governed by the AnimeSchedule.net Privacy Notice. Managing or deleting your AnimeSchedule account is done on their website.

Your choices and control

  • Sign out — signing out in the app revokes the app's access token with AnimeSchedule.net and removes it from your device.
  • Revoke access — you can also revoke AniTimer's access at any time from your AnimeSchedule.net account settings.
  • Delete everything local — deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Children

AniTimer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Use of AnimeSchedule.net accounts is subject to AnimeSchedule.net's own age requirements.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. The history of changes is publicly visible in the policy repository.
