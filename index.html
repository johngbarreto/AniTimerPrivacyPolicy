AniTimer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short: AniTimer does not collect, store, or share your personal data on any server of its own. The app talks directly to AnimeSchedule.net, and everything it saves (sign-in tokens, cached images, your list data for the widget) stays on your device. There are no analytics, no ads, and no trackers.

Who we are

AniTimer is an independent iOS client for AnimeSchedule.net. It shows the public weekly timetable and anime details from their API. If you sign in, it can also read and update your AnimeSchedule list and profile through OAuth2, after you consent on their site. AniTimer does not create its own account. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by AnimeSchedule.net, and it is not an official or partner app.

Data the app accesses

AniTimer accesses the following data through the official AnimeSchedule.net API:

  • Public anime data — weekly timetables, anime details, posters, and search results. This does not involve any personal data.
  • Your AnimeSchedule account data (only if you sign in) — your anime list (statuses, episode progress, scores, notes), your public profile avatar, banner, and list statistics.

Signing in is optional. Without signing in, the app only fetches public anime data.

How sign-in works

Sign-in uses the industry-standard OAuth2 authorization flow (with PKCE) provided by AnimeSchedule.net. Your AnimeSchedule password is entered only on the AnimeSchedule website inside a secure system browser session — AniTimer never sees or stores your password. The app receives an access token, a refresh token, and your AnimeSchedule user id. Those values are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only. The app does not sync or back them up to a server of its own.

List data that is not public (status, episode progress, scores, and dates) is used only to show and edit your own list, including on the Home Screen widget on the same device. AniTimer does not share that data with other users.

What is stored on your device

  • Sign-in tokens and user id — in the iOS Keychain, until you sign out.
  • Cached images — anime posters cached on the device for a short time to reduce network usage.
  • Widget data — a copy of the anime you are tracking, plus small poster images, in the App Group container group.anitimerwidget so the Home Screen widget can show upcoming episodes.
  • Local notifications — optional air-time alerts scheduled on this device. They are not sent through a server we operate.
  • Preferences — dark mode, home filters, widget air type, and notification lead time, stored in UserDefaults on the device.

The timetable request includes your device’s timezone so week boundaries match your calendar. That timezone is not saved as a separate account setting. Cached data is kept only as long as the feature needs it. Deleting the app removes the local copies.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate our own servers — no data about you is ever sent to us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking SDKs of any kind in the app. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework or an advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data to train AI or machine learning models.

Third-party service: AnimeSchedule.net

All anime and account data comes from AnimeSchedule.net, and requests made by the app go directly from your device to their servers. Their handling of your account and data is governed by the AnimeSchedule.net Privacy Notice. That notice describes the website, including any analytics, cookies, or vendors they use there. Those practices are theirs. AniTimer does not run them inside the app.

Managing, downloading, or deleting your AnimeSchedule account is done on their website, from profile settings. Email contact@animeschedule.net for anything the site cannot do.

Your choices and control

  • Remove titles — swipe a title off My List, or clear it from the anime detail screen. That updates your AnimeSchedule list.
  • Sign out — Profile → sign out revokes the app’s access token with AnimeSchedule.net, deletes the tokens and user id from the Keychain, and removes the widget copy and scheduled air-time notifications from this device.
  • Revoke access — you can also revoke AniTimer’s access from your AnimeSchedule.net account settings.
  • Delete everything local — deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Deleting an account

AniTimer has no account of its own to delete. To stop using the app and remove the data it can reach:

  1. Remove titles in the app (My List, or the anime detail screen).
  2. Sign out in Profile. That clears the Keychain session and the local widget data.
  3. Download or permanently delete your AnimeSchedule account from profile settings on animeschedule.net. Their privacy notice describes that process. Email contact@animeschedule.net for anything the website cannot do.

Age

AnimeSchedule accounts are for people 13 and older. AniTimer is intended for the same audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you are a parent and believe a child has used AnimeSchedule through this app, contact AnimeSchedule at contact@animeschedule.net and open an issue on the policy repository below.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. The history of changes is publicly visible in the policy repository.
